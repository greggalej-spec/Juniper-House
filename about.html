A Letter to Families

Welcome to Juniper House

Choosing a home for someone you love is one of the most important decisions a family will ever make. It is a decision rooted in care, in trust, and in the hope that they will be safe, comfortable and treated with the dignity they deserve.

At Juniper House, we built our community around exactly that promise. We bring together compassionate, attentive caregiving with physician-led medical oversight, so that every resident is supported not just in body, but in spirit. Our team gets to know each person as an individual — their history, their habits, their favourite stories — because real care begins with truly knowing someone.

Whether your family is exploring long-term residential care, a short recovery stay, or simply wants peace of mind, we invite you to take a closer look at what makes Juniper House feel less like a facility, and more like home.

With warmth,
The Juniper House Family

Who We Are

Residents are not patients to be managed, but people to be known.

Juniper House was founded on a simple belief: that growing older should never mean losing dignity, independence, or joy. Every member of our team — from our nursing staff to our caregivers and recreational coordinators — is guided by the same philosophy.

We take the time to understand each resident's medical needs, personal preferences and life story, so that the care we provide feels personal, not procedural. Across our Orange Field and Cascade locations, families trust us to provide a safe, nurturing and beautifully maintained environment where their loved ones can continue to live well — with the right balance of support, independence and community.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional, compassionate and medically guided residential care that enables older adults to live with dignity, comfort and purpose in a safe and nurturing environment.

Our Vision

To become the Caribbean's leading network of assisted living communities, setting the benchmark for excellence in elderly care through compassion, innovation and clinical expertise.
