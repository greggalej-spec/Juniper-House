A Gentle Reflection

Is it time to consider assisted living?

Some common signs families notice:

  • Frequent falls
  • Medication confusion
  • Memory loss
  • Social isolation
  • Caregiver burnout
  • Difficulty preparing meals
  • Needing assistance with daily activities
“Choosing assisted living is not a step away from love — it is an act of love. It means making sure the people who cared for us are, in turn, cared for: safely, gently, and with dignity.”
