Visitor Guidelines

  • Valid photo ID must be presented upon arrival.
  • All visitors must sign the visitor logbook.
  • Only visitors on the resident's approved visitor list will be permitted entry.
  • Visitors should respect the privacy and comfort of all residents.
  • Children must remain under adult supervision.
  • Additional visitors may be approved for birthdays and special occasions.
