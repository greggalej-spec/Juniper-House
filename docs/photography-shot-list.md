# Juniper House — Photography Shot List

A prioritized list of photographs the business should commission (or capture on a
good phone in bright natural light). Shots marked **[replaces placeholder]**
directly swap out AI-generated temporary images currently on the site — the
placeholder files are all prefixed `placeholder-` in `assets/img/` and flagged
with an HTML comment where they're used.

## General guidance

- Shoot in the **morning (8–10 a.m.) or late afternoon (4–6 p.m.)** — soft, warm
  light suits the brand. Avoid harsh midday sun and indoor fluorescent-only shots.
- **Landscape orientation** for exteriors and common areas (websites need wide
  images); portrait is fine for rooms and details.
- Tidy each space first; add small warm touches (fresh flowers, a folded throw).
- For any photo featuring residents or staff, get **written consent** first.
- Highest useful resolution; no filters. Send originals, not WhatsApp-compressed
  copies (use "send as document" or email).
