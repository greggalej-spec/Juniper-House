Visiting & Family

Staying connected

Yes, during designated visiting hours: 10:00 a.m. – 12:00 noon and 3:00 p.m. – 6:00 p.m. Additional visitors may be approved for birthdays and special occasions.

Yes, once the appropriate sign-out procedures have been completed.

Please bring valid photo ID and sign the visitor logbook on arrival. Only visitors on the resident's approved visitor list are permitted entry, and children must remain under adult supervision. Full guidelines are on our Contact page.

Care & Medical

How we look after residents

Yes. Medication administration is part of our nursing team's daily care, alongside vital signs monitoring and chronic disease management.

Yes. Residents may continue seeing their own physician or choose to use physicians affiliated with Juniper House.

Yes. Dementia care is one of our core services, delivered with patience and attentive support.

The caregiver immediately notifies the nursing supervisor, who assesses the resident and contacts the designated family member and physician. Whenever possible, care is managed safely within the facility; if hospital care is required, emergency medical services are contacted and the resident is transferred promptly.

Yes. Meals accommodate medical conditions and dietary requirements — including diabetic-friendly, low-sodium and texture-modified diets.

Rooms & Living

Making it feel like home

Yes. Accommodation for couples is available, with pricing provided upon request based on room availability and care requirements.

Yes. Residents may bring televisions, mini refrigerators and personal belongings provided they do not create safety concerns or inconvenience another resident. In shared rooms, personal items must not interfere with the comfort of the roommate.

Orange Field (Carapichaima) starts at TTD $4,000 per month; Cascade, our premium villa residence, starts at TTD $7,000 per month. Both offer shared and private room options.

Stays & Admission

Getting started

Yes. Alongside long-term residential care we offer short-term respite stays — including recovery after illness or surgery and family caregiver respite — and a Day Stay programme with structured activities, companionship and daytime supervision, returning home each evening. See Care & Services.

Five gentle steps: an enquiry to share your loved one's needs, a tour of Orange Field or Cascade, a full assessment of medical and personal care needs, a personalised care plan, and move-in. Details on our Admissions page.

Everyday clothing, toiletries, current medications and aids (glasses, hearing aids, walking aids), and personal touches like family photographs or a favourite blanket. The full checklist is on our Admissions page.
