Welcome to Juniper House

Care built around the person, not just the condition.

Juniper House was founded on a simple belief: that growing older should never mean losing dignity, independence or joy.

We are a physician-led assisted living community, built by people who understand that exceptional senior care lives at the intersection of clinical expertise and genuine compassion. Our team takes the time to understand each resident's medical needs, personal preferences and life story — so the care we provide feels personal, not procedural.
